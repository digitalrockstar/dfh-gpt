## 2026-11-03 Store POS (offline first)
- Model: a device (one browser acts as one billing device) holds a block of invoice numbers reserved by the server (100 at a time, refilled when 20 are left), bills against it with no connection, and pushes bills later with idempotency keys. The server recomputes every bill and answers accepted, review, duplicate or rejected per bill. A rejected bill stays on the device with the reason.
- Device (frontend/src/pos_store.js, page_pos.js): IndexedDB holds catalog (about 1,100 active SKUs), device, invoice block and the bill queue. Taking the next invoice number and saving the bill happen in one transaction, so a crash cannot skip or repeat a number. Service worker (frontend/sw.js) keeps the app shell, so a reload with no network still opens the POS. Manifest added, so it installs as an app. "Simulate offline" switch on the screen for the demo. Sync runs on reconnect, every 20 seconds, after each bill and on the Sync now button. Synced bills stay on the device for 14 days.
- Billing: search, barcode Enter, category chips; loose items by weight (kg box and 100 g, 250 g, 500 g, 1 kg buttons), packs by count; line discount; split payment across cash, UPI and card; optional customer phone with a WhatsApp consent tick (creates a customer row on sync); receipt with print. Prices are MRP inclusive of GST; tax is worked out inside the price at the assumed rates (loose and packed rates per HSN). Rounded to the rupee, round off shown. The same integer maths is in dfh/pos_calc.py and pos_store.js; the browser test shows the server accepting device totals.
- Server rules (dfh/pos_api.py): invoice must belong to the device series and sit inside the granted block, no reuse; bill time cannot be more than a day ahead; total must match the server within Re 1; payments must add up to the total; weight cap 50 kg a line. Flagged as review, not rejected: a unit price more than 5% under list, or a line discount above 10%. The customer was already charged, so the bill is kept and shown to a manager. A device cannot hold more than 500 unused numbers.
- Returns (online only, needs the original bill): defective or damaged only, 10 days from the bill date, quantity limited to what is left to return, refund pro rata on the line. A cashier raises it as pending; store manager, CEO or admin approves or rejects; a manager entering one approves it in the same step. Refunds are treated as cash refunds in the day close.
- Day close (online, per store and date): cash sales, approved refunds and cash drops against opening cash and the counted cash. Variance above Rs 100 needs a note and the day closes as "variance". It is blocked on the screen while bills on the device are unsynced. A bill that syncs after its day was closed is accepted and written to the audit log.
- Sync health: per device last sync, bills and rejects in 24 hours, state ok (within 1 hour), late, stale (over 24 hours) or never. Store manager sees the own store; CEO, finance, admin and procurement see the chain. The three planted stale stores (F5) show at the top.
- Seed (dfh/seed/pos.py): 50 bills over 14 days for the HSR Layout store (two devices) with payments, so returns and the day close have something to work on. One bill is older than the return window. Series blocks are reset, so the first claim starts after the seeded bills.

## 2026-11-02 Module 6 Demand and replenishment
- Forecast (dfh/demand/model.py, rebuild alone with `python -m dfh.demand.run`, about 12 s): per store and SKU, 8 weekly buckets from 1 Nov, 795k rows with an 80% band and value at list price. Level is the trailing 56 day average with stockout days and new store ramp removed, each day divided by its festival factor, shrunk toward the same SKU in the same store tier. Festival factor is 1 + category sensitivity x calendar intensity. Sensitivity is learned from chain sales by regression (July onward, with trend and weekday terms), not typed in.
- Learned sensitivity: Gift boxes +300%, Chocolates +199%, Dates +148%, Exclusives +146%, Nuts and dry fruits +118%, Berries +54%, Spices +47%, Seeds +26%. Chain forecast peaks in the week of 1 Nov at about 2x the no-festival level and returns to base after Diwali.
- Backtest (cut at 3 Oct, scored on the last 4 weeks, stockout weeks and 25 new stores left out): error by city and category 7.4% against 18.8% for a flat average; warehouse and SKU 23.0% against 29.7%; store and SKU 49.2% against 51.9%. Store and SKU is mostly Poisson noise at low volume, so do not quote it as a headline. The 80% band covered 86% of cells over Rs 200 on weeks it was not fitted on.
- Known weaknesses: the forecast runs 7% low across the board because it trails a rising trend (no trend term). The festival shape in the model is the same shape the calendar uses, so on real data the score will be worse. No festival is modelled after Diwali, so Christmas and New Year are flat.
- Buying (dfh/demand_api.py): packs roll up to their bulk SKU in grams at 100% yield, gift boxes roll up to components. Order up to demand over (lead time + 21 day order cycle) + safety stock (z 1.65 for the top quarter by value, 1.28 for the rest, error taken from the backtest) against on hand + on order. Cheapest current vendor, MOQ and lead time from the price book. 264 open suggestions worth about Rs 12.9 Cr: 205 critical (stock ends before a new order can land), 32 order this week, 27 plan.
- Stock position is a modelled snapshot (table stock_position), not real. It is the swap point: Module 4 ledger replaces on hand, Module 3 purchase orders replace on order. Vendor cost is before freight and duty until Module 3.
- Planted findings now visible: F3 Pune warehouse is critical on almonds and cashews; F7 gift box components are short at five warehouses, 20 of 85 gift boxes sit below 90% coverage, Pistachios the usual limiter.
- Screen: KPIs, sales with forecast and band, festival sensitivity, buy suggestions with approve and reject (orders above Rs 25 lakh need CEO, finance or admin; you can send an adjusted quantity through the API, the screen approves the suggested one), gift box components, accuracy card with assumptions, store min and max review with accept. Control Tower shows two new exceptions (critical buy lines, gift boxes short).
- Roles: warehouse sees only its own warehouse and cannot approve; store manager sees forecast and min/max for the store only, no buy suggestions; cashier has no access.
